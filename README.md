# Evaluation of Lactate and ALP in Bone Health & Patient Recovery

## Project Overview
This project evaluates the clinical diagnostic utility of serum Lactate and Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP) as biomarkers for assessing bone health and monitoring recovery trajectories among patients at Federal University Teaching Hospital Lafia.

## Objectives
- Quantify baseline Lactate and ALP levels across patient recovery stages.
- Perform statistical analysis to evaluate correlation between biomarker concentration and patient recovery times.
- Provide data-driven visual summaries to support clinical interpretation.

## Tools & Methodologies
- **Data Processing:** Data cleaning, missing value imputation, outlier handling.
- **Statistical Methods:** Descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, inferential testing.
- **Tools Used:** Python (Pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib/Seaborn), IBM SPSS, Excel.

## Key Findings & Outputs
- Identified significant variations in ALP activity corresponding to distinct phases of bone healing.
- Produced summary statistical tables and comparative distribution plots for clinical review.
